## 摘要
The convergence of artificial intelligence (AI) and health care offers unprecedented opportunities to address global disparities in access to specialized epilepsy diagnosis and treatment, particularly in resource-limited settings. Advances in AI models, combined with increasing internet connectivity and smartphone usage in low-income and middle-income countries, offer promising solutions for epilepsy diagnosis and monitoring through AI-based analysis of electroencephalography recordings captured with affordable open-source systems, smartphone data and electronic health records. However, this technological revolution raises important questions about the preservation and expansion of clinical expertise, AI algorithm transparency and health-care equity. In this Review, we examine how AI can help to address disparities and improve global epilepsy care while maintaining high clinical standards and addressing systemic challenges.
